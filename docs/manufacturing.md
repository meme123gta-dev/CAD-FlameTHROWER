# Manufacturing Notes

## Process

FDM, 0.4 mm nozzle assumed.

## Recommended Materials

- PETG for general prototype shells that may see handling outdoors
- PLA for visual-only indoor silhouette prototypes

## Print Orientation

- **Module halves:** Prefer the flat split face on the build plate (mirrors).
- **Full modules:** Only on large-format beds; otherwise use halves.
- Barrel halves are long (~420 mm) — may need further length segmentation later.

## Wall Strategy

Default walls are 3.0 mm for a large empty shell. Increase for abuse resistance after fit checks.

## Joints (future)

Rev A does not finalize screws, clips, or sealing between modules. Dry-fit halves and
modules before designing fasteners.

## Prototype Label

PROTOTYPE ONLY — STRUCTURAL CAPACITY NOT VERIFIED

## Safety

Print and assemble as a theatrical/display water-gun shell only. Do not adapt this
geometry into fuel, ignition, combustion, or weapon systems.
